# Recording the 1–3 minute demo

Use the fictional sample, never a real class chat. On Windows 11, open the app and press **Windows + Shift + R** to select a screen area in Snipping Tool, or open Snipping Tool and choose **Record**. Keep the recording between one and three minutes. The reviewers need to see the app functioning; slides alone do not meet the requirement.

1. Start at the app's empty state. Click **Xem tình huống mẫu**. Let the OCR finish so the transcript and cited results are visible.
2. Show the **8:00** and **9:00** meeting candidates for **28/09/2026**. Click a **Xem nguồn** link and show the corresponding line in the screenshot. Point out that calendar export is disabled while the times conflict.
3. Explain that a real parent must confirm with the teacher; **9:00 is only a demonstration selection**, not a claim about which message is correct. Select 9:00.
4. Tick the pen/notebook preparation item, show the submission deadline and its source, then click **Tải lịch nhắc đã xác nhận**. Show the downloaded `.ics` file; if possible, import it into a calendar to show the chosen meeting and deadline. Do not claim an automatic calendar sync.

The attached `english-captions.srt` can be imported into a video editor and aligned with the actual clicks. It is a template translation, not an already recorded or uploaded video. Upload the finished video publicly to YouTube or Vimeo and check it opens without signing in. Provide its URL on the Devpost submission form.
