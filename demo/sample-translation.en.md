# English translation of the fictional sample screenshot

The screenshot shown in `public/sample.png` is fictional. Its Vietnamese text is translated below in reading order; names are fictional. Dates use day/month/year.

| Original Vietnamese | English translation |
| --- | --- |
| Nhóm lớp 2A | Class 2A group |
| Cô Hương · 17:20 | Teacher Hương · 5:20 p.m. (chat timestamp) |
| Họp phụ huynh: 8:00 ngày 28/09/2026, phòng 203. | Parent meeting: 8:00 a.m. on September 28, 2026, room 203. |
| Mang theo bút và sổ tay. | Bring a pen and notebook. |
| Nộp phiếu thông tin trước 27/09/2026. | Submit the information form before September 27, 2026. |
| Ban đại diện · 18:05 | Parent representatives · 6:05 p.m. (chat timestamp) |
| Họp phụ huynh: 9:00 ngày 28/09/2026, phòng 203. | Parent meeting: 9:00 a.m. on September 28, 2026, room 203. |
| Mong phụ huynh đến đúng giờ. | Please arrive on time. |
| TÌNH HUỐNG MẪU — nội dung hư cấu cho bản demo | SAMPLE SCENARIO — fictional content for this demo. |

The 8:00 and 9:00 meeting times conflict. The app displays both source lines and asks the parent to verify the time with the teacher before choosing one. The chat timestamps are not meeting times. The demo's 9:00 choice only illustrates the interface; it does not establish which meeting announcement is correct.
