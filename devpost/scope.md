# Nhắc Đúng Việc

One line: A parent turns a screenshot of class notices into an attributed checklist and a confirmed reminder calendar.

## The Unique Kernel
When two messages name different hours for the same parent meeting, the app displays both original source lines and asks the parent to choose before it creates a calendar event. It does not silently prefer the latest message.

## Who It's For
A parent who receives several class-group announcements and has to turn them into practical preparations and deadlines.

## The Core Loop
Upload a screenshot, inspect extracted meeting time, items, and submission deadline alongside the source lines, resolve a disagreement, check off preparations, and download calendar reminders.

## Inspiration & Identity
The learner described the visual story as “ảnh chụp lộn xộn → phát hiện mâu thuẫn → lịch nhắc đã xác nhận”. No palette or other visual reference was specified.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The learner chose a concrete, understandable demo for the contest's design, idea, and presentation criteria, while explicitly recognizing that prizes are not guaranteed.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A prepared screenshot has two meeting times. OCR extracts both; the interface shows citations to the message lines, flags the conflict, and disables calendar export until a parent chooses a time. The supplies and deadline become checklist entries with source lines.

## The POC Boundary
One image at a time; Vietnamese class notices; meeting date/hour, supplies, submission deadline; user confirmation, local checklist, and downloadable calendar file.

## Later
More varied wording, multiple images, direct calendar account connection, and proactive phone notifications.

## Explicitly Cut
Automatic choice between conflicting times, because the parent must verify it; an account and cloud storage, because they do not help prove this loop; sending messages to classmates or teachers, because that requires authorization and adds another workflow.
